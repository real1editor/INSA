About Merkato & Visit Wolaita

Merkato Wolaita is your one-stop destination for authentic Wolaita crafts, textiles, spices, and local goods. We connect local artisans with the world, preserving Wolaita's rich cultural heritage.

Our marketplace celebrates the vibrant traditions of Wolaita Sodo—from handwoven Kuta cloth and Buluko dresses to artisan clay pots and pure wild honey.

Our Vision

To empower local communities by providing a modern, trusted platform that honors tradition while embracing technology.

Help Center

How do I place an order?

Browse products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. We accept mobile money and cash on delivery.

What are the delivery options?

We deliver nationwide across Ethiopia. FREE delivery on orders over $200 within Wolaita Sodo.

How can I return an item?

Contact our support team within 7 days of delivery. Returns are accepted for unused items in original packaging.

Are my payments secure?

Yes, we use encrypted payment gateways and never store your card details.
