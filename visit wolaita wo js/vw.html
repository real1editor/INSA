Welcome to Wolaita Zone

A Region of Rich History and Breathtaking Views

The Gateway to Southern Ethiopian Splendor

Situated in Southern Ethiopia, Wolaita Sodo and its surrounding countryside form an extraordinary tapestry of green hills, ancient royal history, vibrant marketplaces, and traditional architecture.

Whether trekking Mount Damota, exploring the geothermal springs near Lake Abaya, or participating in the vibrant Gifata New Year festival, Wolaita offers an authentic and unforgettable journey.

  • Capital: Wolaita Sodo
  • Topography: Mountainous Highlands & Rift Valley Basins
  • Key Highlights: Mount Damota, Ajora Falls, Lake Abaya Shorelines
